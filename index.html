Cloud Sea

An endless sky of floating islands. Fly anywhere, find landmarks, thread ring courses, run missions, and share a room code with friends to fly, race and explore together.

Plane colour
Steer
Move the mouse, or use WASD / arrow keys. Up climbs.
Land
Hold Ctrl to slow right down, then touch a lake or airstrip gently. Shift and pull up to take off again.
Speed
Shift faster, Ctrl slower, Space boost. Boost drains its meter and refills when you let go; rings top it up.
Phone
Left stick steers; Faster, Boost and Slow buttons on the right. On foot they become Wave, Run and Jump.
Gamepad
Left stick steers, right trigger faster, left trigger slower, A boost
Friends
In a room: 1 to 5 (or the Say button) send quick messages, G pings the map, Race starts a countdown for everyone, and every few minutes a shared hunt for eight gold balloons begins.
Dragon
The Ride button swaps your plane for a dragon.
Villagers
Land by an airstrip, walk to a villager and press F to take an errand. Errands unlock hats in the Pause menu.
On foot
F gets out or in. W/S walk, A/D turn, Shift run, Space jump, Q wave.
Other
N opens the big map, T skips time ahead, M mutes, P pauses. If it stutters, set Graphics to light.
